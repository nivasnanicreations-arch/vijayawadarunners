RUNNER ESSENTIALS

Need personal help? Email info@vijayawadarunners.com

The landmark 10th Edition of Vijayawada Marathon is scheduled for Sunday, 6 December 2026. The flagship holding area and start gates open at 04:00 AM IST, with flag-offs commencing from 05:00 AM IST along the scenic Krishna Riverfront promenade and Prakasam Barrage. Staging venue details will be released in the official Runner Guide.

You can choose between three distinct running experiences:

  • 21.1 KM Half Marathon: Premier chip-timed distance for seasoned runners and endurance athletes (Age: 18+).
  • 10 KM Timed Run: Electronic RFID timing challenge ideal for intermediate runners striving for personal bests (Age: 15+).
  • 05 KM Fitness & Joy Run: Untimed celebration of movement for families, students, and first-time runners (Age: 8+).

Bib collection takes place exclusively at the official Vijayawada Marathon Expo held on Friday & Saturday (4–5 December 2026), 10:00 AM to 06:00 PM. Every participant must present their confirmation email and a valid government ID proof. Note: Bibs will strictly not be distributed on race morning.

To ensure runner safety and reopening of arterial city roads in coordination with traffic authorities:

  • 21.1 KM Half Marathon: 3 hours 30 minutes from flag-off.
  • 10 KM Timed Run: 1 hour 45 minutes from flag-off.
  • 05 KM Joy Run: 1 hour 15 minutes from flag-off.

All registered participants receive:

  • 10th Edition Commemorative Technical Running T-Shirt.
  • Official Bib with RFID timing chip (for 10K & 21.1K participants).
  • On-course hydration, electrolytes, energy fruits, and full medical coverage.
  • 10th Milestone Heavy Metal Finisher Medal upon crossing the finish line.
  • Post-race hot nutritious breakfast and recovery support.
  • Downloadable digital timing certificate and official event photographs.

Athlete health is our foremost priority. The 10th Edition route is monitored by 6 fully equipped Cardiac Life Support ambulances, 12 rapid mobile paramedical motorbikes with AEDs, 8 hydration-medical posts spaced every 1.5–2 KM, and a 40-bed recovery clinic with certified sports physiotherapists at the finish village.

Yes. A secure baggage holding counter is provided at the main holding village for registered runners. Please avoid leaving valuable electronics or jewelry. Designated runner vehicle parking zones will be marked on the route map and communicated via email.

Registrations are non-refundable and strictly non-transferable. Bib numbers and RFID chips are encoded with participant medical info, age group category, and emergency contacts. Running under someone else's bib is a safety violation and leads to disqualification.
