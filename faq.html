FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ATHLETE LOGISTICS GUIDELINES

Click any question below to expand the full logistical instructions.

Where and when do I collect my official Race Bib & Timing Kit?
The 10th Landmark Bib Expo will take place at the Benz Circle Convention Hall, MG Road, Vijayawada on Friday, December 4 and Saturday, December 5, 2026 from 10:00 AM to 07:00 PM IST. Please bring your government photo ID and registration SMS/email. Note: There is strictly no Bib distribution on Sunday race morning!
Is there a secure Baggage Drop counter at Prakasam Barrage?
Yes! Secure baggage holding counters will open at 04:00 AM near the Prakasam Barrage North Head. Please use the official baggage tag provided in your Bib kit. Ensure all items are packed in a single bag. We advise runners not to leave laptops, cash, or valuable jewelry.
Where can runners park their vehicles on Race Morning?
Designated parking zones have been established with AP Police:
• P1 (Punnami Ghat Grounds): Reserved for 21.1K Half Marathoners.
• P2 (Bhavanipuram Stadium): Reserved for 10K & 05K participants.
Dedicated free electric shuttle buses will ferry runners to the Barrage flag-off arch every 8 minutes starting from 03:45 AM.
What are the strict course cut-off times for each category?
To ensure vehicular traffic can resume safely on the Prakasam Barrage, official cutoffs are:
• 21.1K Half Marathon: 3 hours 30 minutes (Course closes at 08:30 AM).
• 10K Timed Challenge: 1 hour 45 minutes (Course closes at 07:45 AM).
• 05K Fitness Joy Run: 60 minutes (Course closes at 07:30 AM).
Sag wagons will follow the course to safely extract any runners unable to make the time cutoff.
When and how do I receive my Official RFID Timing Certificate?
Instant preliminary finish time SMS will be sent to your registered mobile number within 20 minutes of crossing the finish mat. Official certified digital diplomas featuring your split pacing, overall rank, and age-category standing will be available for download on our portal from 12:00 PM on race day.
Can I transfer my race bib to a friend or request a refund?
Registrations are non-refundable. However, official bib transfer requests to another runner can be submitted through our runner dashboard until November 20, 2026 for a processing fee of ₹150. Running under someone else's bib without official transfer leads to automatic chip disqualification.
