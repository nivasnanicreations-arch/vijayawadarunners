WEEKEND TRAINING SESSIONS

Join Us at the Barrage Every Sunday

Every Sunday morning at 05:30 AM, runners from all chapters assemble at the Prakasam Barrage Gateway for open group training runs. No fees, no registration required — simply lace up and join the pack!

Assembly Time: Every Sunday, 05:30 AM IST
Location: Prakasam Barrage Toll Gate Plaza
Distances: 5K, 10K, and 15K Paced Packs
Support: Free water, electrolytes & stretching
