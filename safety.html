6 Mobile Cardiac ICU Ambulances

Equipped with multi-parameter monitors, defibrillators, oxygen, and emergency resuscitation pharmacology. Stationed at Barrage North, Barrage South, Kanaka Durga Ghat, Tadepalli U-turn, Benz Circle, and Finish Gauntlet.

40 Paramedical Roving Pacers

Certified emergency technicians running alongside the crowd. Equipped with waist-pack AED units, blister care kits, salt tablets, and rapid-response comms to dispatch nearest extraction buggies.

18 Hydration & Salt Hubs

Spaced at strict 1.5 KM intervals. Serving sterile mineralized water, Fast&Up hypoosmolar electrolyte solutions, glucose drinks, iced wet sponges, and fresh orange wedges to combat dehydration.

Misting Showers & Ice Pods

4 overhead misting shower tunnels along the open highway stretch. Ice dunk stations and cold towel compresses stationed at KM 12, KM 16, and KM 19 to mitigate core temperature spikes.

Post-Race Physiotherapy Hub

A 20-bed recovery clinic located immediately past the finish arch. 25 licensed sports physiotherapists offering assisted stretching, quad foam rolling, calf cramp relief, and cold therapy tubs.

Level 1 Emergency Partner

Latha Super Speciality Hospitals maintains dedicated zero-wait emergency bays and standby cardiologists during race morning, with reserved green corridors cleared by AP Traffic Police.

PRE-RACE ADVISORY

PRE-RACE RUNNER HEALTH CHECK

Every runner's well-being is our paramount priority. Please review these essential medical principles before stepping onto the Prakasam Barrage start line:

1. Listen to Your Body (Dizziness & Chest Tightness)

If you experience unusual chest discomfort, radiating shoulder pain, sudden nausea, or lightheadedness, immediately pull over to the road shoulder and alert the nearest volunteer or roving pacer.

2. Hydrate Wisely (Avoid Hyponatremia)

Do not overconsume plain water without electrolytes. Alternate between water and Fast&Up isotonic drinks at the hydration hubs to maintain sodium balance.

3. Fill Out Your Bib Medical Profile

On the reverse of your official race bib, write your emergency contact number, blood group, and any known drug allergies (e.g., penicillin or NSAIDs) in permanent ink.
