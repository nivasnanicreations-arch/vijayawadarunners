THE 4 PILLARS OF RUNNER CARE

ENGINEERED FOR
MAXIMUM SAFETY.

MEDICAL EXCELLENCE

5 Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances stationed on course, 12 medical posts, mobile paramedic cycling units with AED units, and recovery physio tents.

PURE HYDRATION

Hydration stations every 1,500 meters stocked with mineral water, isotonic electrolytes, fresh fruit, glucose, and cold sponges for thermal regulation.

ROUTE MARSHALS

150+ trained course marshals coordinating directly with Vijayawada Traffic Police to secure 100% vehicle-free, barricaded running corridors.

VOLUNTEER FORCE

500+ enthusiastic community volunteers managing smooth bib check-ins, barcode-secured baggage holding, and high-energy encouragement.

GUIDELINES • PREPARATION

RUNNER HEALTH & HEAT SAFETY PROTOCOL

Hydration Strategy

Begin hydrating 48 hours prior to race morning. Do not skip the early hydration kiosks at 1.5K and 3.0K even if you do not feel thirsty.

Listen to Your Body

If you experience light-headedness, chest tightness, or disorientation, immediately alert the nearest marshal or volunteer. Mobile medical cyclists are spaced every 800m.
