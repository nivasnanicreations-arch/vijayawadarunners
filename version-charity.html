Flagship Cause

Prosthetic Limbs & Mobility

An amputee without a prosthetic limb is confined to isolation and economic dependency. We provide high-quality modular artificial legs, calipers, and rehabilitation so beneficiaries can walk, work, and support their families with dignity.

₹5,000 sponsors 1 customized below-knee prosthetic limb.
Environmental Action

#RunClean Krishna River Conservation

Marathons must never leave behind plastic pollution. Vijayawada Runners leads zero-waste marathons and conducts bi-weekly plogging (picking up litter while jogging) drives across Prakasam Barrage, Bhavani Island, and canal banks.

100% compostable paper cups & zero single-use plastic bottles on race day.
Grassroots Athletics

Para-Athletes & Tribal Youth Sponsorship

Talented youngsters from rural Andhra Pradesh often lack running shoes and sports nutrition. We identify gifted runners and provide long-term athletic scholarships, professional training shoes, and travel grants for national trials.

Eco-Athletics

Become a Vijayawada Plogger

Plogging combines jogging with picking up litter (from the Swedish plocka upp). On every Sunday group run, our dedicated plogger squad wears biodegradable gloves and carries eco-bags to clear trash from public parks and riverside trails.

On Marathon Race Day, over 60 student ploggers trail the last runner to leave the streets cleaner than before the event began.

Zero Waste Event: All organic waste is composted at Vijayawada Municipal Corporation facilities.
Free Plogging Kits: Reusable collection bags and safety gloves provided free to all volunteers.
