12-Week Couch to 21.1K Training Syllabus

Designed by certified IAAF and Athletics Federation coaches specifically for Indian weather conditions.

Phase Week Tuesday (Easy Base) Thursday (Tempo / Intervals) Sunday (Long Run) Key Goal
Phase 1: Foundation Week 1-2 3 KM Jog (Zone 2) 4 x 400m Strides + Core 5 KM Easy Conversational Build tendon strength & breathing rhythm
Phase 1: Foundation Week 3-4 4 KM Easy 5 x 400m Pace Intervals 7 KM Long Run Establish aerobic cardiovascular base
Phase 2: Build Week 5-6 5 KM Easy 6 KM Tempo Run at 10K Pace 10 KM Long Run (Hydration Drill) Test nutrition gels & electrolytes
Phase 2: Build Week 7-8 6 KM Easy 7 KM Progressive Pace Run 14 KM Long Run (Prakasam Barrage) Half marathon endurance simulation
Phase 3: Peak Week 9-10 7 KM Easy 8 KM Threshold Tempo 18 KM Peak Long Run Confidence building & gear check
Phase 4: Taper Week 11 5 KM Easy 4 KM Shakeout + Stride 10 KM Easy Taper Glycogen reloading & leg recovery
Race Week Week 12 3 KM Easy Jog 2 KM Light Strides + Stretch RACE DAY: 21.1 KM (VRHM) Execute pacing plan & claim your medal!
