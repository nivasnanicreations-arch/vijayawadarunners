PRAKASAM COURSE

Eco-Plogging Stations

Clean running with zero waste along the sacred Krishna banks. Designated volunteer green zones collect and segregate post-race energy gel wrappers, fruit skins, and cartons for 100% eco-reprocessing.

Zero-Single-Use-Plastic Hydration

18 high-throughput hydration hubs serving biodegradable sugarcane fiber cups, mineralized electrolyte solutions, and iced orange wedges. Continuous flow water refill pods stationed every 1.5 KM.

Advanced On-Route Medical Grid

In partnership with Latha Super Speciality Hospitals: 6 mobile cardiac ICU ambulances, 40 paramedical roving pacers equipped with AEDs, and 3 base triage recovery hubs at Barrage, Highway, and Finish.
