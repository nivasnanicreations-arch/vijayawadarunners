PACER ETIQUETTE

HOW TO RUN WITH A PACER BUS

1. LINE UP EARLY

Look for your target pacer flag in the starting corral 20 minutes before flag-off. Introduce yourself to the pacer and let them know your goal.

2. DON'T CROWD THE FLAG

Maintain a comfortable 2 to 3-meter cushion around the pacers so everyone has fresh airflow and clear line-of-sight to timing mats and road markers.

3. HYDRATION SIGNALS

Pacers will announce each upcoming water station 200 meters in advance. Follow their signal to slow down smoothly and grab water without jostling.

4. THE FINAL KILOMETER SURGE

At KM 20 (or KM 9 in 10K), if you have energy left in your tank, your pacers will urge you to surge ahead for an unforgettable personal best finish!
