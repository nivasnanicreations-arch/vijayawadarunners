01 / OUR MOVEMENT

A DECADE OF
RUNNING TOGETHER.

Founded in 2016 by a handful of sunrise running enthusiasts, Vijayawada Runners has evolved into Andhra Pradesh's premier road running movement.

FROM 50 RUNNERS
TO A CITY REVOLUTION.

A decade ago, running for leisure in Vijayawada was rare. A dedicated group laced up their shoes at 5:00 AM to jog along Prakasam Barrage while the city slept. They had one simple belief: shared strides create unbreakable bonds.

Fast-forward to 2026, as we prepare for the 10th Landmark Edition of the Vijayawada Marathon, our community counts over 6,000 active runners, 6 city training chapters, and has raised ₹45 Lakhs+ for local causes.

Community Pillars

  • 1. Zero Membership Barriers Every morning run, training plan, and pacing session is 100% free for everyone.
  • 2. Inclusive Paces From 7:30 min/km walk-joggers to 3:45 min/km podium chasers, nobody runs alone.
  • 3. Riverfront Pride Championing the beauty and heritage of Vijayawada and the sacred Krishna riverbed.
HISTORIC MILESTONES (2016–2026)

OUR MILESTONE CHRONICLE

2016 • THE GENESIS

Sunrise River Run Across Prakasam Barrage

50 passionate runners gathered for an informal morning run across Prakasam Barrage, planting the seed for what would become Vijayawada Runners.

2018 • SAAP PARTNERSHIP

First Official 10K Timed Road Race

Partnered with Sports Authority of Andhra Pradesh (SAAP) to conduct an official chip-timed 10K with 1,200+ participants and police traffic corridors.

2021 • THE FLAGSHIP HALF MARATHON

21.1 KM Route Along Krishna Riverfront

Inaugurated the 21.1 KM Half Marathon route, turning Vijayawada into a recognized destination on the national road-running map.

2024 • GLOBAL ACCREDITATION

Official AIMS Course Certification

The marathon course was officially measured and accredited by AIMS (Association of International Marathons and Distance Races) for global qualifier timing.

2026 • 10TH LANDMARK EDITION

Celebrating A Decade In Motion (6 December 2026)

Hosting 6,000+ runners across 5K, 10K, and 21.1K categories with ₹3,00,000 cash prize purse, AIMS certification, and celebratory festival arena.
