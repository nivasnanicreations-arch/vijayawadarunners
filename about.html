10TH EDITION LANDMARK

A DECADE OF
RUNNING TOGETHER.

Founded in 2016 by a handful of sunrise running enthusiasts, Vijayawada Runners has evolved into Andhra Pradesh's premier road running movement.

FROM 50 RUNNERS
TO A CITY MOVEMENT.

A decade ago, recreational road running in Vijayawada was rare. A dedicated group laced up their shoes at 5:00 AM to jog along Prakasam Barrage while the city was still asleep. They had one simple belief: shared strides create unbreakable bonds.

As we prepare for the 10th Landmark Edition of the Vijayawada Marathon on 6 December 2026, our community unites thousands of active runners, weekend training chapters, and charitable causes across the region.

“ONE CITY. THOUSANDS OF STRIDES. YOUR PACE. YOUR PEOPLE. YOUR FINISH LINE.”
— Vijayawada Runners Philosophy

Community Pillars

  • 1. Zero Barriers to Movement

    Every weekend morning run and pacing clinic is welcoming and open to everyone.

  • 2. Inclusive Pacing

    From casual walk-joggers to sub-2-hour half marathoners, nobody runs alone.

  • 3. Krishna Riverfront Pride

    Celebrating the sunrise heritage of Vijayawada and our iconic barrage crossing.

HISTORIC MILESTONES (2016–2026)

OUR MILESTONE CHRONICLE

01

2016 · The Genesis

50 sunrise runners gathered for an informal morning run across Prakasam Barrage, planting the seed for Vijayawada Runners.

“One road, fifty hearts, and the sunrise over the Krishna.”
03

2018 · Official Road Races

First official chip-timed road race with civic support, police traffic-free corridors, and expanding participation.

“Bringing professional timing to our hometown roads.”
06

2021 · Flagship Half Marathon Launch

Inaugurated the 21.1 KM Half Marathon route, placing Vijayawada firmly on India's national running calendar.

“Testing endurance on historic river corridors.”
09

2025 · Prelude to the Milestone

Thousands of runners stood at the starting line, setting the stage for our 10th landmark celebration.

“A celebration of discipline, health and unity.”
10

2026 · The 10th Landmark Edition (6 Dec 2026)

A decade in motion. 5 KM, 10 KM, and 21.1 KM Half Marathon with commemorative medals and festival arena.

“RUN THE CITY. FEEL THE MOMENT.”
