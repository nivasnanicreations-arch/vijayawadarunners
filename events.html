COMMON QUESTIONS

EVENT FAQS

Where does the race start and finish?

All three races (5K, 10K, and 21.1K) start and finish inside the historic IGMC Stadium (Indira Gandhi Municipal Stadium), MG Road, Vijayawada.

How will I receive my timing chip bib and race kit?

Bib Expo will be held on Friday, 4 Dec and Saturday, 5 Dec 2026 at IGMC Stadium. You will receive an SMS and email notification with your assigned Bib number and QR code collection pass.

Can I transfer or cancel my registration?

Registration transfers are permitted up to 15 November 2026 with a ₹150 nominal re-tagging fee. Cancellations are non-refundable as event kit orders are locked in advance.
