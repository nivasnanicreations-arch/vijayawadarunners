01 / Our Movement

A Decade of Running

From 50 sunrise runners across Prakasam Barrage to Andhra Pradesh's premier road running movement.

Ten Years of Shared Strides

Founded in 2016 by a dedicated circle of fitness advocates, Vijayawada Runners set out to transform our historic city into a thriving sanctuary for health, cardiovascular longevity, and morning discipline.

Today, as we commemorate our 10th Landmark Edition on 6 December 2026, the Vijayawada Marathon stands as Andhra Pradesh's largest road running movement—welcoming beginners, weekend warriors, and seasoned athletes onto one road to move, improve, and belong.

Official Race Rules & Regulations

Bib Pinned on Chest: Official bib with RFID timing chip must be pinned visibly to the front of the jersey throughout the race.

Cut-off Enforcement: Strict cut-off times apply. Runners behind cut-off will be offered a sweep bus ride for safety.

Non-Transferable: Bib numbers cannot be swapped or transferred to other runners under any circumstances.

No Wheels or Pacing: Personal pacers on two-wheelers or bicycles are strictly prohibited on the runners' corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

When and where is Bib Collection / Expo?
The 2-day Vijayawada Marathon Expo will be held on Friday, 4 December and Saturday, 5 December 2026 (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM) at the Indoor Stadium, IGMC Complex. Please bring your registration confirmation email and government ID proof. No bibs will be distributed on race day morning.
Is baggage deposit available on race day morning?
Yes, secure complimentary baggage counters will be available inside IGMC Stadium from 4:00 AM. Please do not store valuable items, laptops, or cash in your baggage bag.
What official Pacer Buses will run for the 21.1K Half Marathon?
Official pacer buses with flags and timing balloons will be available for 2:00h, 2:15h, 2:30h, and 2:45h finish times. You can run with your target bus without any additional fees.
When can I view my timing results and download my certificate?
Provisional timings will be sent via SMS within 30 minutes of crossing the finish line. Official certified leaderboards and high-resolution downloadable certificates with split times will go live by 2:00 PM on race day at our Results portal.
Is parking available at the venue?
Designated vehicle parking is arranged at PWD Grounds (Swaraj Maidan) adjacent to IGMC Stadium with traffic marshals directing vehicles. We strongly encourage carpooling and reaching by 4:15 AM.
05 / Runner Safety

Safety & Runner Support

Your well-being, health, and race day safety are safeguarded by comprehensive emergency medical infrastructure.

Medical Support

6 Advanced Cardiac ICU ambulances, trauma doctors, and emergency base hospital tents on-course powered by Latha Super Speciality Hospital.

Hydration

Aid stations every 2 KM stocked with isotonic electrolytes, glucose, chilled water, fresh citrus fruit, and cold cooling sponges.

Route Marshals

60+ trained cycling marshals and pilot motorbikes monitoring runners, guiding lead packs, and assisting any runner in distress.

Volunteer Support

400+ energetic volunteers from local colleges and running chapters cheering, directing turns, and managing finish chute medal distribution.

Runner Safety

100% traffic-segregated running corridor planned with Vijayawada Traffic Police, sweep buses, and automated course timing mats.

Our Movement

The Vijayawada Runners Story

Vijayawada Runners is an open, non-profit community trust founded on the simple belief that running unites people, prevents chronic lifestyle diseases, and builds resilience.

We host free weekly morning runs every Saturday and Sunday at 5:00 AM across 6 neighborhood chapters: Benz Circle, BRTS Road, Prakasam Barrage, Bhavanipuram, Gunadala, and KL University. Zero membership fees, pure passion!
