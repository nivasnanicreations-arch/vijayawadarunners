EVENT DAY SCHEDULE

HOUR-BY-HOUR ITINERARY

Strict timeline adherence ensures all categories experience seamless race-day execution and synchronized medical supervision.

04:00 AM

Holding Area & Baggage Drop Opens

Designated baggage holding counters open with bib-tagged security tokens. Secure personal items before entering starting pens.

04:30 AM

Official Warm-Up & Pacer Bus Lineup

Certified physios lead dynamic stretching. Pacer buses hoist target pace flags (1h 45m to 3h 00m) in respective starting corrals.

05:00 AM

21.1K Half Marathon Flag-Off

The flagship endurance wave departs across Prakasam Barrage locks. Cutoff window: 3 Hours 30 Minutes.

06:00 AM

10K Timed Challenge Flag-Off

Intermediate athletes depart on the riverfront speed corridor. Official 10K pacers leading 50m to 75m pacing buses.

06:30 AM

05K Fitness Joy Run Flag-Off

Families, youth, senior citizens and beginner runners start their scenic 5K promenade with celebratory music and cheer stations.

08:30 AM

Podium Awards Ceremony & Cash Prize Presentation

Presentation of ₹2,25,000 cash purse across Open and Age Category podium finishers on the main celebration stage.

BIB & RUNNER KIT EXPO

Bib collection happens strictly at the Pre-Race Expo. NO BIBS WILL BE ISSUED ON RACE DAY MORNING.

Dates: Friday, 04 Dec & Saturday, 05 Dec 2026
Timings: 10:00 AM — 07:00 PM IST
Venue: Vijayawada Club / Stadium Convention Hall
Mandatory Documents: Confirmation Email / SMS with QR code & Government Photo ID.
RACE DAY ESSENTIALS

Check off these items before leaving home on Sunday morning:

  • Official RFID Timing Bib pinned firmly on front chest
  • Official Dry-Fit Event Running Tee / Singlet
  • Tested running shoes and anti-blister socks
  • Hydration belt or reusable handheld bottle
  • Arrive at least 45 minutes prior to category flag-off
