PURPOSE BEYOND THE PODIUM

RUNNING WITH A HIGHER CAUSE

Every kilometer logged in the Vijayawada Marathon creates a ripple effect throughout Andhra Pradesh. Over the past 10 editions, we have mobilized over ₹45,00,000 towards critical social initiatives:

Pediatric Cancer Care Support

Direct surgical sponsorships and nutritional supplements for underprivileged children battling oncology disorders.

Under-18 Track Scholarships

Funding spike shoes, certified coaching, and travel grants for rural sprinters and middle-distance runners.

Sacred Krishna Riverbank Conservation

Bi-weekly plogging squads ensuring the riverfront ghats remain pristine and plastic-free.
